## Dependencies and media

Future software dependencies retain their individual license and notice
requirements, including any eligibility conditions. The choice of a dependency
in an architecture plan does not waive those terms.

External data and media retain their own use, caching, attribution, derivative
work, and redistribution conditions. The
[research methods and rights matrix](docs/architecture/research-methods.md#provider-rights-matrix)
describe preliminary checks and unresolved permissions; the project's
nonprofit status does not automatically grant rights in third-party assets.

Maintainers should add exact installed dependency notices and source-specific
asset notices when those materials are acquired. Publication approval requires
a complete source manifest and actual rights evidence for the selected assets.
