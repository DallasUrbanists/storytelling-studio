## Proposed rendering contract update

The bridge evaluator `evaluateFrame(project, variantId, frame)` takes a
project validated by `parseProject` and returns a deeply frozen JSON frame
snapshot. It rejects noninteger, out-of-range, and unsupported variant inputs.
The snapshot includes the chosen 1080 × 1920 or 1920 × 1080 viewport at pixel
ratio 1, scene ID, absolute camera, focus geometry, all project layer states,
and current-scene annotations. Both viewports use the same saved camera and
geographic anchors. The schema does not yet support authored variant overrides.

Camera and label time are scene-local. Layer visibility and trace time use
absolute project frames, and the evaluator orders layers by saved `order`
then ID. Exact camera keys and held endpoints retain their saved values.
Between keys, the camera interpolates center in Web Mercator using the
shortest wrapped longitude, zoom linearly, and bearing along the shortest
turn. Both longitude and bearing take the positive direction at a half-turn
tie. The bridge supports flat ground, pitch 0, and linear easing.

A trace is empty at its start frame and complete from its exclusive end
frame. It uses cumulative spherical rhumb-line distance along the saved
Mercator segments. A partial endpoint advances by geographic distance along
the segment as drawn. A Polygon trace follows only the exterior.
The complete polygon, including holes,
remains available for fills. A label fades linearly between the saved
entrance and exit boundaries, steps when a fade has zero duration, and has
opacity 0 outside its half-open interval.

Camera fitting, cubic easing, authored bearing direction, variant camera
and label overrides, terrain, globe, roll, and nonzero pitch remain outside
the bridge schema. Multipart geometry, antimeridian-spanning geometry,
interior-ring tracing, timed fill/dimming, styled callout layout, and
resource, basemap, credit, and roadway state also remain outside this
evaluator. Keep browser projection and pixel agreement in the future
acceptance criteria until both hosts supply evidence.
